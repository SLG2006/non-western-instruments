## 2. Audio clips
The files in `audio/` are excerpts of recordings by other people. Each clip keeps the license its author chose.
The license, its URL, the author and the source page of every clip are in `metadata.csv` and `credits.csv`.

| License | Clips |

Every clip was changed: cut to a 10-second excerpt, mixed to mono, saved as 16-bit WAV, and for short
recordings zero-padded to 10 seconds. Freesound clips were decoded from the Freesound preview. The credit line of
each clip states the changes. When you use or redistribute a clip, give its credit line. Clips under a
NonCommercial license (CC BY-NC) may not be used for commercial purposes.

No clip in this release is under a NoDerivatives or ShareAlike license, Freesound's Sampling+ license, or from an
uploader whose Freesound generative-AI preference is "no-gen-ai".
